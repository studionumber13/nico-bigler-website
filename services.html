How it works / 02

A clear process. No black box.

01 / Brief

We talk sound first

A short call about your track, your release, your audience. I need to hear the music before I design anything. You get a fixed quote within 48 hours.

02 / Concept

Directions, not drafts

You receive two distinct concept directions with rationale: what each one says, and why it fits your sound. You pick one; we sharpen it.

03 / Execution

Craft and iteration

Full execution of the chosen direction, with two structured revision rounds. You see progress, not silence.

04 / Delivery

Everything, ready to ship

Final files in every format you need: streaming, print, social, animated. Plus a short usage note so the artwork stays sharp wherever it lands.

Questions / 04

Before you ask.

Who owns the artwork after the project?

You receive full usage rights for the agreed purpose: release, promotion, merch if specified. I retain the right to show the work in my portfolio and archive. Exclusive buy-outs are possible and quoted separately.

What do you need from me to start?

Tell me what the project is and which service you're after, when you need it by, and give me something to hold on to, a few style ideas or references you love and hate. For a release, send the track if you have it, even a rough mix. We sort out the rest together.

Can you work with tight release deadlines?

Often, yes. Rush delivery inside one week is possible depending on my schedule, with a rush surcharge. Ask early. The earlier you write, the more options we have.

Do you only work with drum and bass artists?

No. The practice is rooted in DnB and underground club culture, but the approach of translating sound into image works across genres and for brands that want to connect with music culture credibly.

How are payments handled?

50% deposit to book the slot, 50% on delivery of final files. Invoiced from Switzerland (CHF or EUR). Fixed quotes only. No hourly surprises.
